The goal

A recruitment platform needed its job and company pages understood as structured entities, not just as pages of text.

What was built

JSON-LD schema for job postings, organisations and the pages connecting them, plus sitemaps that expose those pages cleanly to crawlers.

The result

Pages now describe themselves in a language search engines and AI systems read directly, which is the groundwork for rich results and accurate answers.
