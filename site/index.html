FAQ

Questions before you book a call?

Short, straight answers. Anything else, ask on the call.

What do you do?

I help brands get found on Google and named in AI answers. That covers technical SEO, GEO, AEO, entity SEO, content strategy and local or international setups.

Who do you work with?

SaaS companies, service businesses, clinics and online brands, mostly in the USA, UK, MENA and Europe. Startups and established teams both.

What’s the difference between SEO, GEO and AEO?

SEO gets your pages ranked. AEO shapes them so answer boxes and voice results lift them directly. GEO makes your brand the source AI assistants trust enough to cite. The best results come from doing all three on the same foundations.

Can you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No, and anyone who does is guessing. What I do promise: honest diagnosis, fixes ranked by impact, and clear reporting so you can see exactly what moved.

How soon will I see results?

Technical fixes often show up within weeks. Content and authority work usually takes a few months to compound. You get a realistic timeline after the audit, not before.

How do we get started?

Book a free audit call. You get a first read on what is holding the site back and whether working together makes sense.
